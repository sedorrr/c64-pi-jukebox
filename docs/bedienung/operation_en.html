This guide shows how to operate the jukebox (version 1.22). Installation, hardware and all details are in its manual – in the web page under the tab Help (every round ? button jumps to the right place) and as docs/MANUAL.md. The pictures show sample data.

1 · Three ways to operate it

How to get to the web page:
  1. Open http://c64pi.local/ in the browser. If the name does not work (on some Android phones), use the Pi's IP address – it is shown on the TV next to the QR code (just scan it with the phone's camera) and on the Pi's display.
  2. Log in with the web password (factory setting commodore64 – a yellow bar reminds you to change it under Settings → Web password).
  3. As an app on the phone: on an iPhone in Safari Share → Add to Home Screen, on an Android phone in Chrome ⋮ → Add to Home screen. The jukebox gets an icon of its own (the note) and opens without an address bar like an app – no app store needed.

The jukebox keeps playing on its own, even when no page is open. All open pages show the same state – start on the phone and carry on at the PC.

English or German: on the first visit the jukebox takes the browser's language. To change it, use Settings → System → Sprache / Language or Sprache / Language in the TV menu; the language applies to the page, the TV menu, the displays and the manual.

2 · The web page

At the very top is always the current tune, with the ⏻ button to the right of it: Switch TV on / off (over HDMI-CEC), switch the selected Ultimate on and off, and Switch off jukebox (see section 12). Below are the tabs – on the phone partly as symbols:

Updates: once a day the jukebox looks for a new version on GitHub. It installs only when you say so – with Install in the notice, under Settings → System → Updates or in the TV menu (Check for update). This takes a few minutes; settings, lists and song lengths are kept, and if something goes wrong the old program comes back.

Short messages (“Saved”, “Commando – playing”) appear at the bottom for a few seconds.

3 · Choosing music – tab “Files”

  • Sources are tiles at the top: a network share (NAS, Windows, Mac), an FTP server, the USB stick on the Ultimate or a USB stick on the Pi. Tap to open, ✎ edits, + Source sets up a new one (with “Search the network”). The jukebox remembers the last folder of each source.
  • Tap a folder to open it; the path line above leads back with one tap.
  • Tap a SID (or ▶) to play it – and after it the other files of the folder. The current list is replaced.
  • + appends a file to the list without interrupting playback.
  • ▶ Play folder takes all SIDs of the folder – with subfolders from all subfolders too, with Shuffle mixed. + List appends them instead.
  • Filter field: works on the open folder at once (file name, title, composer, year; several words must all match, e.g. hubbard 1986).
  • 🔍 Subfolders searches the word in all subfolders (up to 500 hits); “Play folder” then plays the hits, ✕ in the path line leads back.
  • 🔍 HVSC searches the whole HVSC at once, without reading folders (as soon as the HVSC song lengths are loaded). It finds title and composer from the files once Settings → Song lengths → Read titles for the search has run.
  • Yellow 2 SIDs / 3 SIDs marks stereo tunes.
  • Song lengths and STIL: in the HVSC folder C64Music/DOCUMENTS tap load as HVSC lengths at Songlengths.md5 and load as STIL at STIL.txt. The jukebox then changes exactly at the end of a tune and shows background notes on the tunes.

  • Progress bar: with a known length to the end, otherwise to the time limit (“max. 5:00”).
  • ⚑ Mark end here appears while a tune without a known length plays: press it at the end of the tune – the playing time becomes its length, and from then on it plays exactly that long.
  • ⊘ Never play again skips to the next tune and puts the current one on the block list (see section 5).
  • Suggestions: if the jukebox finds out by itself where a tune starts again, or finds the same tune in the HVSC, it offers to use that length (accept or No).
  • Below the title: the SID chips, the STIL notes and, if needed, a SID socket change (“socket 2 at $D420 for this tune”).

The play list

  • Play: double-tap or double-click (on the PC also Enter).
  • Select: tap; on the PC with Ctrl/⌘ several, with Shift a range.
  • ⋯ at a tune (or right-click): play, play next, choose sub-tune, own length, favourite, never play again, show in the Files tab, remove from the list.
  • Play next puts the selected tunes right after the current one; Remove takes them out.
  • Tap ★ to make a tune a favourite; the ★ tick above the list shows favourites only.
  • Sort: tap a column header; on the PC drag rows with the mouse.
  • Search: the field above the list searches file name, title and composer.
  • Lengths in the column: 3:05 from the HVSC, 3:05* own, (5:00) grey = unknown (the time limit applies). Check lengths looks for missing lengths in the HVSC.
  • Save … stores the list under a name (see tab “Lists”).
  • More ▾: import/export a play list of the PC jukebox, Discover (a rarely played tune), Stop after … (tunes or minutes), select all, clear the list.
  • Keys on the PC: Space play/pause, ← → previous/next tune.

5 · Lists – tab “Lists”

Saved play lists

▶ Load replaces the current list and plays it, + appends it, ↗ exports it for the PC jukebox, ✎ renames, 🗑 deletes. Lists are saved in the tab ▶ with Save ….

Build lists

One tap plays at once: ★ Favourites, Most played, Never played, Random from the HVSC, Short tunes, Tunes for 2 or 3 SIDs. Under Own selection from the HVSC you can choose by area (musicians, games, demos), composer, length, number of SIDs, STIL notes and “never played”. This needs the HVSC song lengths loaded from a source.

Recently played

The last 100 tunes with the time, newest first. ▶ plays one next, + appends it, Use as play list turns the history into a new list, Clear history empties it.

Statistics

How often what was played – counts and favourites belong to the file, also in other lists.

Blocked tunes

Tunes blocked with ⊘ Never play again (tab ▶, the ⋯ menu of a row or the TV menu). The jukebox skips them when moving on, with shuffle, “Discover” and in the built lists; in the play list they are crossed out. Chosen by hand they play anyway. Play again lifts the block.

6 · Devices – tab “Devices”

  • Ultimates: name and Ethernet address of each Ultimate (Search the network finds them), the network password if set. active = this one plays. Don't forget Save.
  • ⏻ On / Off: Off stops playback and switches the Ultimate off, On wakes it over its Wi-Fi (Ultimate 64-II). The jukebox learns the Wi-Fi MAC for this itself. The ⏻ button at the top of every page does the same.
  • Picos and stream distributor: the Pi passes the Ultimate's sound on to visualizers and audio receivers and sends them titles and retro news – setup see the guide to the C64 ecosystem.
  • Devices on the network: all Picos and the Pi with the way of the sound; add as Pico adds a device to the distributor's list.

7 · Display: TV and screen – tab 📺

The tab has three groups: Picture on the TV (HDMI, background, retro news), Sound (sound on the TV, Bluetooth headphones) and Controls (TV remote, display on the Pi).

Picture on the TV

  • Show a picture on the Pi's HDMI output switches the output on. Every change applies at once – you see on the TV right away how an effect looks.
  • Effect: one of 17 (as in the PC jukebox) or “background only”.
  • Overlays: title, SID chips and playing time; STIL notes at the bottom left; a QR code when nothing plays; the big “Now playing” card for every new tune (duration adjustable).
  • Hide effects when no music plays (after 20 s).
  • Change effect automatically: tick effects and change during playback – a different one every 180 s.

Background: none, pictures (slide show: upload them or choose a folder of a source, e.g. on the NAS; change every 20 s with cross-fade) or the C64 picture live from the Ultimate. “Background brightness” dims it.

Retro news on the TV: headlines from retro pages (Indie Retro News, amiga-news.de, Commodore News …) when idle as a big card, as a ticker at the bottom during the music. Every source can be switched on and off, own feeds with + Source.

Sound on the TV

Sound over HDMI to the TV plays the Ultimate's sound through the TV. Volume (0–100 %) makes it quieter without looking for the remote. With fade out at the end of a tune, in at the next the tunes blend gently (only on the TV; the Ultimate itself plays unchanged). If a hi-fi is connected to the Ultimate as well, turn up only one of them – the TV is about 0.2 s later.

Bluetooth headphones

Instead of the TV, the sound can also play through Bluetooth headphones (or a Bluetooth speaker):

  1. Put the headphones into pairing mode (usually hold the power button for a few seconds).
  2. Tap Search for headphones, then Pair at the device found.
  3. From then on the sound goes to the headphones as soon as they are connected, and back to the TV when you switch them off. Headphones that are switched on reconnect by themselves.

The HDMI output must be switched on for this. Volume and fading apply to the headphones too; on the TV, Picture and sound → Sound via headphones switches over.

Controls

TV remote control (HDMI-CEC): operate the jukebox with the TV's remote – see section 10. Also when the TV switches on and off: when the music starts, when the Pi starts, after a while without music – and whether the jukebox switches off when the TV goes off (see section 12).

Display on the Pi: type of display (small OLED or 2.8″ with touch), set up touch, rotate the picture, after how many minutes it goes dark – see section 11.

  • Schedule: play a list or stop at fixed times, e.g. weekdays at 18:00 “Evening” – with the TV on/off if wanted.
  • Song lengths: upload or remove the HVSC file, download own lengths, Read titles for the search (for 🔍 HVSC); remove STIL.
  • Take over data from the PC jukebox: play counts, favourites and own lengths.

  • Change the web password; download and restore a backup (all settings, sources, lists, own lengths and the block list in one file).
  • Switch off and smart plug: switch off the jukebox, set up a smart plug – see section 12.
  • Updates: look once a day (on/off), Check now, Install.

9 · On the TV

On the HDMI output the Pi shows effects to the music at 1920 × 1080 – over black, a slide show or the C64's live picture. Top right are title, composer, the SID chips needed, year and sub-tune, bottom right the playing time. For every new tune the “Now playing” card appears for a moment.

Some of the 17 effects

SID voices shows the three voices of each SID for the last 5 seconds (pitch upwards, voice 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, brighter = louder), on the right note, waveform and level. For this the Ultimate's “Debug Stream Mode” (under Data Streams) must include a 6510 part (default “6510 Only”), and it does not work together with the background “C64 picture”.

10 · Remote control and menu on the TV

Switch on: tab 📺, card TV remote control (HDMI-CEC), tick “Control the jukebox with the TV's remote control”. CEC must be on at the TV (called e.g. Anynet+, SimpLink, Bravia Sync, VIERA Link). Most TVs send the keys only to the device they are showing – so switch to the Pi's input (or use Switch TV to the Pi now in the card).

11 · Touch display on the Pi

  • Buttons: ⏮ previous tune, ■ stop (or ▶ play when nothing plays), ⏭ next tune, ⏻ on / off. A pressed button flashes briefly.
  • On / Off: TV on and TV off (over HDMI-CEC; dark when the TV remote control is switched off), Switch off jukebox (red – only a second tap within 5 s switches off, the button then says “Tap again”) and Back. Top right shows whether the TV is on; after 30 s without a tap it returns to playback.
  • A tap outside the buttons changes to the next page for 30 s: playback → levels → network.
  • After 10 minutes without playback it goes dark (adjustable); the first tap only wakes it, the second one operates.
  • The touch reacts to pressure – best tap with a fingernail or a stylus. Set up touch (tab 📺, card “Display on the Pi”) shows three crosses to tap if taps land beside the buttons.
  • In the Pi's own Wi-Fi the display shows name, password and address in large letters.

12 · Switching on and off

Switching off the jukebox

Always shut the Pi down cleanly – simply cutting the power can damage the SD card. It works from everywhere:

WhereHow
Web page⏻ button at the top → Switch off jukebox, or Settings → Switch off and smart plug
TVmenu → Switch off → Switch off jukebox (asks first)
Touch display⏻ → Switch off jukebox, tap twice

With a smart plug

If the Pi is plugged into a Tuya smart plug (app “Smart Life” or “Tuya Smart”), it sets the plug's switch-off timer before shutting down: after Power off after seconds (default 30) the power goes off. It is switched on with the app or by voice, e.g. “Alexa, turn on Jukebox” – the Pi starts as soon as there is power. To set it up, enter the plug's Device ID and Local key (where to find them is in the manual) and tap Check.

Together with the TV

In the card TV remote control (tab 📺):

  • Switch the TV on and to the Pi when the Pi starts: the TV comes on and shows the Pi (in the first 90 s after the start).
  • Switch off the jukebox when the TV goes off: one minute after the TV goes into standby the Pi shuts down, with a smart plug the power goes off afterwards – not while Bluetooth headphones are connected.
  • Also: switch the TV on when playback starts; switch it off when nothing has played for a while.

So “Alexa, turn on Jukebox” is enough for the Pi and the TV, and in the evening the TV's remote switches everything off again.

Ultimate

Switch it on and off with the ⏻ button at the top, in the tab “Devices” or in the TV menu (Ultimate or Switch off).
