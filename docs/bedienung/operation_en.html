This guide shows how to operate the jukebox. Installation, hardware and all details are in its manual (German) – in the web page under the tab Hilfe (every round ? button jumps to the right place) and as docs/HANDBUCH.md. The web page and the TV menu are in German; buttons and menu items are quoted here as they appear, in italics with a translation. The pictures show sample data.

1 · Three ways to operate it

WithWhat you can doRequirement
Web page
phone, tablet, PC		 Everything: choose and play music, lists, settings, TV, Ultimates, Picos. browser in the same network, address http://c64pi.local/
The TV's remote control Play, stop, change tune and sub-tune, change the style – and through the menu on the TV choose music, shuffle, lengths, choose the Ultimate, switch it on/off. Pi on an HDMI input, HDMI-CEC switched on at the TV
Touch display on the Pi

How to get to the web page:
  1. Open http://c64pi.local/ in the browser. If the name does not work (on some Android phones), use the Pi's IP address – it is shown on the TV next to the QR code (just scan it with the phone's camera) and on the Pi's display.
  2. Log in with the web password (factory setting commodore64 – a yellow bar reminds you to change it under Einstellungen → Web-Passwort).
  3. Tip for the phone: “Add to Home Screen” in the browser menu – the jukebox then opens like an app.

The jukebox keeps playing on its own, even when no page is open. All open pages show the same state – start on the phone and carry on at the PC.

3 · Choosing music – tab Dateien

  • Sources are tiles at the top: a network share (NAS, Windows, Mac), an FTP server, the USB stick on the Ultimate or a USB stick on the Pi. Tap to open, ✎ edits, + Quelle (add source) sets up a new one (with a network search). The jukebox remembers the last folder of each source.
  • Tap a folder to open it; the path line above leads back with one tap.
  • Tap a SID (or ▶) to play it – and after it the other files of the folder. The current list is replaced.
  • + appends a file to the list without interrupting playback.
  • ▶ Ordner abspielen (play folder) takes all SIDs of the folder – with mit Unterordnern (with subfolders) from all subfolders too, with Zufall (shuffle) mixed. + Liste appends them instead.
  • Filter field: works on the open folder at once (file name, title, composer, year; several words must all match, e.g. hubbard 1986).
  • 🔍 Unterordner searches the word in all subfolders (up to 500 hits); “play folder” then plays the hits, ✕ in the path line leads back.
  • Yellow 2 SIDs / 3 SIDs marks stereo tunes.
  • Song lengths and STIL: in the HVSC folder C64Music/DOCUMENTS tap als HVSC-Längen laden (load as HVSC lengths) at Songlengths.md5 and als STIL laden at STIL.txt. The jukebox then changes exactly at the end of a tune and shows background notes on the tunes.

  • Progress bar: with a known length to the end, otherwise to the time limit (“max. 5:00”).
  • ⚑ Ende hier merken (remember the end here) appears while a tune without a known length plays: press it at the end of the tune – the playing time becomes its length, and from then on it plays exactly that long.
  • Suggestions: if the jukebox finds out by itself where a tune starts over (“Fängt nach 1:35 von vorn an”), or finds the same tune in the HVSC, it shows Übernehmen (accept) / Nein.
  • Below the title: the SID chips, the STIL notes and, if needed, a SID socket change (“Sockel 2 für dieses Stück auf $D420”).

The playlist

  • Play: double-tap or double-click (on the PC also Enter).
  • Select: tap; on the PC with Ctrl/⌘ several, with Shift a range.
  • ⋯ at a tune (or right-click): play, play next, choose sub-tune, own length, favourite, show in the files tab, remove from the list.
  • Als Nächstes (next) puts the selected tunes right after the current one; Entfernen removes them.
  • Tap ★ to make a tune a favourite; the ★ tick above the list shows favourites only.
  • Sort: tap a column header; on the PC drag rows with the mouse.
  • Search: the field above the list searches file name, title and composer.
  • Lengths in the column: 3:05 from the HVSC, 3:05* own, (5:00) grey = unknown (the time limit applies). Längen prüfen (check lengths) looks for missing lengths in the HVSC.
  • Speichern … saves the list under a name (see tab “Listen”).
  • Mehr ▾ (more): import/export a playlist of the PC jukebox, Entdecken (discover a rarely played tune), Stopp nach … (stop after a number of tunes or minutes), select all, clear the list.
  • Keys on the PC: Space play/pause, ← → previous/next tune.

5 · Lists – tab Listen

Saved playlists

▶ Laden (load) replaces the current list and plays it, + appends it, ↗ exports it for the PC jukebox, ✎ renames, 🗑 deletes. Lists are saved in the tab ▶ with Speichern ….

Ready-made compilations

One tap plays at once: ★ Favoriten (favourites), Meistgespielt (most played), Nie gespielt (never played), Zufällige aus der HVSC (random from the HVSC), Kurze Stücke (short tunes), Stücke für 2 oder 3 SIDs (tunes for 2 or 3 SIDs). Under Eigene Auswahl aus der HVSC (own selection) you can choose by area (musicians, games, demos), composer, length, number of SIDs, STIL notes and “never played”. This needs the HVSC song lengths loaded from a source.

Recently played

The last 100 tunes with the time, newest first. ▶ plays one next, + appends it, Als Wiedergabeliste übernehmen turns the history into a new list, Verlauf löschen clears it.

Statistics

How often what was played – counts and favourites belong to the file, also in other lists.

6 · Devices – tab Geräte

  • Ultimates: name and Ethernet address of each Ultimate (Im Netz suchen – search the network – finds them), the network password if set. aktiv (active) = this one plays. Don't forget Speichern (save).
  • ⏻ Ein / Aus (on/off): Aus stops playback and switches the Ultimate off, Ein wakes it over its WLAN (Ultimate 64-II). The jukebox learns the WLAN MAC for this itself.
  • Picos and stream distributor: the Pi passes the Ultimate's sound on to visualizers and audio receivers and sends them titles and retro news – setup see the guide to the C64 ecosystem.
  • Devices in the network: all Picos and the Pi with the way of the sound; als Pico eintragen (add as Pico) adds a device to the distributor's list.

7 · Display: TV and screen – tab 📺

TV (HDMI)

  • Bild am HDMI-Ausgang des Pi zeigen (show a picture on the Pi's HDMI output) switches the output on. Every change applies at once – you see on the TV right away how a style looks.
  • Effekt (style): one of 17 (as in the PC jukebox) or “background only”.
  • Overlays: title, SID chips and playing time; STIL notes at the bottom left; a QR code when nothing plays; the big “now playing” card for every new tune (duration adjustable).
  • Hide styles when no music plays (after 20 s).
  • Change style automatically: tick styles and während der Wiedergabe wechseln – a different one every 180 s.

Background: none, pictures (slide show: upload them or choose a folder of a source, e.g. on the NAS; change every 20 s with cross-fade) or the C64's picture live from the Ultimate. “Brightness of the background” dims it.

Sound on the TV

Ton über HDMI zum Fernseher plays the Ultimate's sound through the TV. Volume (0–100 %) makes it quieter without looking for the remote. With am Titelende aus-, beim nächsten einblenden the tunes fade into each other (only on the TV; the Ultimate itself plays unchanged). If a stereo is connected to the Ultimate as well, turn up only one of them – the TV is about 0.2 s later.

Retro news on the TV

Headlines from retro pages (Indie Retro News, amiga-news.de, Commodore News …): in idle mode as a big card, as a ticker at the bottom during the music. Every source can be switched on and off, own feeds with + Quelle.

Display on the Pi

Type of display (small OLED or 2.8″ with touch), set up touch, rotate the picture, after how many minutes it goes dark – see section 11.

  • Timer: play a list or stop at fixed times, e.g. weekdays at 18:00 “Feierabend” – with the TV on/off if wanted.
  • Song lengths: upload or remove the HVSC file, download own lengths; remove STIL.
  • Take over data of the PC jukebox: play counts, favourites and own lengths.
  • WLAN and network: choose a WLAN (for the Pi without a cable), the Pi's own WLAN; party mode for away from home without a router.
  • Change the web password; download and restore a backup (all settings, sources, lists and own lengths in one file).

9 · On the TV

On the HDMI output the Pi shows styles to the music at 1920 × 1080 – over black, a slide show or the C64's live picture. Top right are title, composer, the SID chips needed, year and sub-tune, bottom right the playing time. For every new tune the “now playing” card appears for a moment.

Some of the 17 styles

SID voices shows the three voices of each SID for the last 5 seconds (pitch upwards, voice 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, brighter = louder), on the right note, waveform and level. For this the Ultimate's “Debug Stream Mode” (under Data Streams) must include a 6510 part (default “6510 Only”), and it does not work together with the background “C64 picture”.

10 · Remote control and menu on the TV

Switch on: tab 📺, card TV-Fernbedienung (HDMI-CEC), tick Jukebox mit der Fernbedienung des Fernsehers bedienen. CEC must be on at the TV (called e.g. Anynet+, SimpLink, Bravia Sync, VIERA Link). Most TVs send the keys only to the device they are showing – so switch to the Pi's input (or use Fernseher jetzt auf den Pi umschalten in the card).

Notes appear discreetly above the playing time, e.g. “Fängt nach 1:35 von vorn an · OK: als Länge übernehmen” (starts over after 1:35 · OK: take as length) – OK then opens the menu at the right item, a second OK accepts.

In the card TV-Fernbedienung you can also set the TV to switch on and change to the Pi when the music starts, and to switch off when nothing has played for a while.

11 · Touch display on the Pi

  • Buttons: ⏮ previous tune, ■ stop (or ▶ play when nothing plays), ⏭ next tune.
  • A tap outside the buttons changes to the next page for 30 s: playback → levels → network.
  • After 10 minutes without playback it goes dark (adjustable); the first tap only wakes it, the second one operates.
  • The touch reacts to pressure – best tap with a fingernail or a stylus. Touch einrichten (set up touch; tab 📺, card “Display am Pi”) shows three crosses to tap if taps land beside the buttons.
  • In the Pi's own WLAN the display shows name, password and address in large letters.

12 · Quick help

What you seeWhat helps
c64pi.local does not workUse the IP address: QR code on the TV or the Pi's display, otherwise in the router's list.
The remote does nothingSwitch the TV to the Pi's input (or “Fernseher jetzt auf den Pi umschalten”); switch CEC on at the TV; check the tick in the card “TV-Fernbedienung”.
No sound on the TV, styles do not moveThe sound comes as a stream from the Ultimate: is the Ultimate on and entered with its Ethernet address? Are Data Streams allowed in the Ultimate?
Tunes always run 5 minutesLength unknown: load the HVSC song lengths (tab Dateien), press “Ende hier merken” at the end or accept the suggestions.
A voice is missing in a stereo tuneSwitch on “switch SID addresses when needed”; ⚠ in the column “SIDs” marks tunes that do not match the Ultimate's setting.
SID voices: a note instead of notesSwitch on “Debug Stream Mode” at the Ultimate; not together with the background “C64 picture”.
Password forgottensee the manual, chapter “Passwort vergessen” (reset over SSH).
